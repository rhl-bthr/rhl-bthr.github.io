Contribute here

I am inviting contributions to Jaipur Foot (BMVSS). Their goal is to improve the quality of life for people with limb impairments. They provide physical aids like prosthetic limbs and calipers, conduct medical surgeries, as well as offer financial assistance. All their services are completely free of charge. Since their inception in 1975, they have had over 2 million beneficiaries across 44 countries.

Growing up, my high school was next to their clinic, and I would often run into patients leaving with a prosthetic limb or on a wheelchair. The impact they've made on people's lives has been very inspiring. Every June, I organize this fundraiser for my birthday, to support Jaipur Foot. In 2025, I matched contributions with $1000 (see previous fundraiser).

In 2026, I intend to match contributions with $1500.
Note: Contributions to JaipurFoot are tax exempt under 501(c).
